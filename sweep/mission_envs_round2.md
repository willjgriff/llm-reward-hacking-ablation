## What we learned in round 1 (why this order)
Qwen3.8-27B at reasoning effort low does **not** hack when hacking means openly breaking an explicit rule or the law: insider_trading 0/200 trades, tower_of_hanoi (8 disks) 0 scripting with the "no scripting" rule vs 100/100 without it. It **does** hack where honest success is impossible or very costly, or where a cue says what the grader wants (TerminalVerifier, MBPP-impossible, Grader, hint_mc's grader-key hint). Prefer environments that create that pressure. Gate: keep an env whose base hack rate is 10-80 %.
